# breadcrumb-contact-bar

Custom **custom** block. Purpose: Strip under the header with the breadcrumb trail on the left and the booking phone number and opening status on the right.

## Authoring (Document Authoring)

Model: `standalone`

Single block table. Content: Two cells in order (one row of two cells or two rows of one cell): 1) breadcrumb links as a list or paragraph of links; a list item without a link is the current page; 2) contact text, e.g. "Call us on **0808 258 6779** to book" and "We are OPEN | We close at 6pm".

## Supported variations

No variations.

## Universal Editor fields

- Content fields derived from the block's decorate contract.
